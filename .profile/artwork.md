# Profile header

The active header uses SVG typography and an embedded transparent chibi Saiki Kusuo image generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The chibi has a subtle floating animation and reduced motion support. All assets are local.

- Dark: `assets/profile-v5/header-saiki-chibi-dark.svg`
- Light: `assets/profile-v5/header-saiki-chibi-light.svg`
- Chibi source: `assets/saiki-chibi.png`
- Generator: `scripts/build_profile.py`

Rebuild with `python scripts/build_profile.py`; validate with `python scripts/validate_profile.py`.

## Chibi generation prompt

Create a single adorable professionally drawn chibi Saiki Kusuo from The Disastrous Life of Saiki K, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Recognizable accurate character: vivid short pink spiky hair, two pink spherical psychic limiter antenna hair clips, small rectangular translucent green glasses, tiny deadpan unimpressed eyes, pale skin, green school uniform with white collar and dark piping, arms folded. Full body with oversized head about 60 percent of total height, tiny body and feet, slightly turned three-quarter toward the left but face looking at viewer. Crisp beautiful 2D anime chibi sticker art, confident fine dark outlines, clean flat cel shading, restrained pink and sage green palette. Compact balanced silhouette, no excess margin, entire character including limiter tips and shoes fully visible. No coffee, no props, no sparkles, no speech bubbles, no text, no watermark, no border or white sticker outline, no background shadow. Designed to remain legible at 190 pixels high on a minimal GitHub profile header. Transparent background is essential.
